# Tomato Grading Rubric

## Grade A — Premium
- Fully ripe, uniform red colour
- No visible blemishes, cuts or bruises
- Firm to touch, correct size (60–80 mm diameter)
- Maximum defect percentage: 0%

## Grade B — Standard
- Mostly ripe, minor colour variation allowed
- Minor surface blemishes (< 10% of surface)
- Slight softness acceptable
- Maximum defect percentage: 10%

## Grade C — Economy
- Uneven ripeness or significant colour variation
- Visible blemishes, small cuts or bruises (10–30% of surface)
- Some softness
- Maximum defect percentage: 30%

## Rejected — Unsaleable
- Rotten, mouldy, or severely pest-damaged
- Large cuts, deep bruises, or foreign matter present
- Very soft or leaking
- Defect percentage: > 30%
